Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions our patients ask.

How do I book an appointment? ➕

You can book an appointment online by visiting our Booking Page or by calling us at 0800 123 4567. Our team is available 24/7 to assist you.

What services do you offer? ➕

We offer a wide range of services including GP services, dental care, mental health support, vaccinations, eye care, and emergency care. Visit our Services Page for more details.

Is my personal information secure? ➕

Yes, your personal information is fully secure and confidential. We follow strict NHS data protection guidelines and never share your information with third parties without your consent.

How much does a consultation cost? ➕

Many of our services are NHS funded and completely free. For private services, prices are clearly displayed on our Services Page. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

What if I need to cancel my appointment? ➕

You can cancel or reschedule your appointment by calling us at 0800 123 4567 at least 24 hours in advance. We understand that plans change and we're here to help.
